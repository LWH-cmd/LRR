# Attribution

The inherited framework builds on [GNNDelete](https://github.com/mims-harvard/GNNDelete), under MIT. Its 2023 Harvard copyright and license are retained in `LICENSE`. `contracts/` contains source-bound helpers from the final research implementation, recorded in `manifests/source_bindings.json`. The two `control_models/` files are unchanged project model sources used by the published operator control; their hashes are in `manifests/operator_control_sources.json`.

Raw datasets are obtained from their upstream sources, linked in `data/README.md`; their original terms and dataset citations apply. No dataset or complete external baseline repository is relicensed as owned LRR code. Historical source paths inside configuration/result records are provenance; root scripts use portable repository-relative paths.
